# University Admissions Bot

## Project Overview
The University Admissions Bot is an AI-based chatbot developed to help students
understand university admission requirements in a clear and simple manner.
Admission guidelines are often lengthy and confusing, which makes it difficult
for students to quickly find important information.

This chatbot addresses the problem by compressing admission-related content
such as eligibility criteria, required documents, and application steps
into short and easy-to-understand responses.

## Problem Statement
Students often struggle with complex admission procedures due to unclear
and scattered information on university websites.
This leads to confusion, errors during application, and missed deadlines.

## Solution Approach
The University Admissions Bot acts as a virtual admissions assistant.
Users can ask questions related to admission processes, and the bot
responds with summarized and simplified answers using text compression techniques.

## Target Audience
- Students applying for undergraduate programs
- Students applying for postgraduate programs
- First-time applicants seeking guidance

## Outcome
The project demonstrates how AI-powered chatbots can simplify real-world
problems in the education domain by improving accessibility and clarity
of information.
